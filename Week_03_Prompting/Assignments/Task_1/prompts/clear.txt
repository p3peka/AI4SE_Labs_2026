Example:
Question: List Italian restaurants in Berkeley with a rating above 3.25.
SQL:
SELECT l.HOUSE_NUMBER, r.NAME
FROM RESTAURANT AS r
JOIN LOCATION AS l ON r.ID = l.RESTAURANT_ID
WHERE LOWER(r.CITY_NAME) = 'berkeley'
  AND LOWER(r.FOOD_TYPE) = 'italian'
  AND r.RATING > 3.25;
